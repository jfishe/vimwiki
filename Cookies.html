Chocolate Chip Cookies

chocolate chip

Ingredients:

  • 114 g softened butter
  • 114 g beef tallow
  • ¼ c white sugar
  • 1-¼ c brown sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 2 eggs
  • 2-¼ c all purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1-½ c special dark chocolate chips
  • ½ c semi-sweet chocolate chips

Directions:

  1. Cream sugar and fats in stand mixer
  2. Add vanilla and eggs.
  3. In another bowl, mix flour, soda and salt.
  4. Add dry ingredients to wet and mix on low speed.
  5. Mix chocolate chips in by hand.
  6. Refrigerate covered for 3 hours
  7. Small cookie scoop onto parchment covered, baking sheet.
  8. Bake 10 minutes at 350 °F pre-heated oven.
