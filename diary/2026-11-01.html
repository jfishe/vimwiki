Arroz con Leche

rice leche milk pudding recipe

Ingredients

Rice

  • 4 cups (800 g) long-grain white rice, rinsed until water runs clear
  • 10 cups (2.4 L) water
  • 4 cinnamon sticks
  • Peel of 2 oranges, wide strips, no white pith (optional)
  • 1 tsp kosher salt

Milks

  • 1 gallon (3.8 L) whole milk, divided
    • 12 cups to cook
    • 4 cups reserved for thinning
  • 4 cans (14 oz / 397 g each) sweetened condensed milk
  • 4 cans (12 oz / 354 ml each) evaporated milk
  • 2 tbsp vanilla extract

Toppings (optional, on the side)

  • 1 cup raisins
  • 1 cup dried cranberries, roughly chopped
  • Ground cinnamon for dusting

Instructions

1. Soften the Rice

  1. In a large stockpot (12 qt or larger), combine:
    • Rice
    • Water
    • Cinnamon sticks
    • Orange peel (if using)
    • Salt
  2. Bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer, and cover.
  3. Cook 20-25 minutes until the water is nearly absorbed and the rice is very soft (softer than for a side dish).

2. Add the Milks

  1. Remove the orange peel.
  2. Stir in:
    • 12 cups whole milk
    • Sweetened condensed milk
    • Evaporated milk
  3. Simmer uncovered over medium-low heat 25-35 minutes, stirring often and scraping the bottom, until the rice is tender enough to crush with the tongue and the mixture is creamy but still soupy.
  4. Remove from heat, remove the cinnamon sticks, and stir in the vanilla. It thickens as it cools, so stop while it looks thinner than desired.

3. Plump the Dried Fruit

  1. Cover raisins and chopped cranberries with boiling water (or hot milk).
  2. Soak 15-20 minutes until very soft, then drain.
  3. Serve in a bowl beside the crock pot so each diner can choose.

4. Hold and Serve

  1. Transfer to slow cooker(s) on Warm or Low.
  2. Stir every 30 minutes. Add reserved milk ½ cup at a time to keep it runny.
  3. Ladle into cups, dust with ground cinnamon, and top with plumped fruit if desired.

Notes

  • 35 servings
  • Runny consistency aka Mexican style.
  • Serve warm from a crock pot, with Thanksgiving breakfast.
  • Nut-free. Rice is cooked extra soft and fruit is plumped and optional, for diners without teeth.
  • Yield is about 24 cups (~⅔ cup per serving). Use two 6-qt slow cookers or one 8-qt or larger.
  • Make ahead: cook the day before, cool quickly, and refrigerate. Reheat in the slow cooker on Low 2-3 hours, stirring in reserved milk to loosen.
  • Scorches easily once the milk is added; use a heavy pot and keep stirring.
  • Prep Time: 10 minutes
  • Cook Time: about 1 hour
