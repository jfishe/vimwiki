Recovering Cholula Hot Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1-½ cups water
  • 1 cup white vinegar
  • 20 g dried chiles de árbol, stems and seeds removed
  • 5 g dried Pequín peppers (or more, to taste)
  • 1 Tb ground paprika
  • 1 tsp roasted garlic powder
  • ½ tsp toasted onion powder
  • ⅛ tsp cumin
  • ¼ tsp Mexican oregano
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 12 g salt
  • 0.1–0.2 wt-% xanthan gum

Directions

  1. Preheat a dry skillet over medium heat.
  2. Toast the dried peppers for 20–30 seconds per side.
  3. Transfer to a sauce pan.
  4. Add water, vinegar and spices.
  5. Soak until peppers soften.
  6. Heat over medium heat to 180 °F (82 °C); keep it at that temperature for about 10 minutes.
  7. Cool slightly, vent blender, and blend until smooth.
  8. Strain and weigh sauce.
  9. Return to blender on low and slowly sprinkle 0.1 wt-% xanthan gum.
  10. Wait 30 minutes and adjust thickness, if needed, with 0.1–0.2 g xanthan gum.
  11. Reheat to 180 °F (82 °C), pour into sterilized bottles, cap right away, and turn the bottle upside down for a couple of minutes.
  12. Refrigerate.

Notes

  • 1,000–3,600 Scoville Heat Units (SHU)
  • Yield is 450–500 g.
