French Apple & Herb Roasted Turkey with Spiced Apple Cider

turkey apple cider herb

Ingredients

Turkey

  • 1 whole turkey (25 lb / 11.3 kg)
  • 6-8 apples, quartered
  • 2 large onions, quartered
  • 8 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 orange, halved (optional but recommended)
  • 12 tbsp (170 g) unsalted butter, softened
  • 3 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 3 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 tbsp fresh rosemary, finely chopped
  • 2-3 tsp kosher salt (17 g)
  • 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper

Roasting Pan

  • 1½ cups spiced apple cider
  • 2½ cups chicken stock
  • 3-4 apples, sliced
  • 4 shallots, halved
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar

Apple Cider Pan Sauce

  • Reserved pan drippings
  • ½ cup chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 4 tbsp cold butter
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

1. Prepare the Turkey

  1. Remove the turkey from the refrigerator 1 hour before cooking.
  2. Preheat the convection oven to 300°F (150°C) Convection Roast.
  3. Remove giblets and pat the turkey completely dry.
  4. In a bowl combine:
    • Softened butter
    • Dijon mustard
    • Thyme
    • Rosemary
    • Salt
    • Pepper

2. Season the Turkey

  1. Gently loosen the skin over the breast without tearing it.
  2. Spread about half of the herb butter under the breast skin.
  3. Rub the remaining butter mixture over the entire turkey.
  4. Fill the cavity with:
    • Quartered apples
    • Onions
    • Garlic
    • Orange halves (if using)
  5. Tie the legs loosely with kitchen twine.

3. Prepare the Roasting Pan

  1. Arrange in the bottom of a large roasting pan:
    • Sliced apples
    • Shallots
  2. Pour in:
    • 1½ cups spiced apple cider
    • 2½ cups chicken stock
    • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  3. Place the turkey on a roasting rack above the vegetables and liquid.

4. Roast

  1. Place turkey in the preheated oven.
    • Total cooking time: approximately 4-4¾ hours
    • Begin checking temperature at 3½ hours
  2. Baste with pan juices every 45-60 minutes.
  3. If the breast becomes deeply golden before the turkey is finished, tent loosely with aluminum foil.
  4. Insert an instant-read thermometer into the thickest part of the thigh without touching bone. The turkey is done when:
    • Breast reaches 165°F (74°C)
    • Thigh reaches 170-175°F (77-79°C)

5. Rest

  1. Transfer turkey to a carving board.
  2. Tent loosely with foil and let rest for 30-45 minutes

Apple Cider Pan Sauce

While the turkey rests:

  • Strain the pan drippings into a saucepan.
  • Skim excess fat.
  • Add:
    • ½ cup chicken stock
    • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • Simmer until slightly reduced, about 10 minutes.
  • Remove from heat.
  • Whisk in 4 tbsp cold butter a little at a time.
  • Season with salt and pepper.

The sauce should be lightly savory with gentle apple and spice notes, not sweet.

Notes

  • Convection oven temperature: 300°F (150°C)
  • Start checking internal temperature at 3½ hours.
  • Target temperatures:
    • Breast: 165°F (74°C)
    • Thigh: 170-175°F (77-79°C)
  • The orange helps balance the sweetness of the spiced cider.
  • The apple cider vinegar keeps the pan sauce savory and bright.
  • Serves: 12-16
  • Prep Time: 30-45 minutes
  • Cook Time: 4-4¾ hours
  • Resting Time: 30-45 minutes

References

  • USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service. Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart.
  • Butterball Turkey Roasting Guidelines.
  • America's Test Kitchen. Turkey Roasting Techniques.
  • Jacques Pépin and other classical French poultry roasting methods.

Turkey Bone Broth

turkey stock

Ingredients

  • 1 turkey carcass (bones, skin, and leftover bits of meat)
  • 1 large onion, quartered
  • 2 carrots, roughly chopped
  • 2 celery stalks, roughly chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp whole black peppercorns
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 12-16 cups cold water

Instructions

  1. If the turkey bones are not already roasted, roast them at 400°F (200°C) for 30-40 minutes until browned.
  2. Place the turkey bones, onion, carrots, celery, garlic, bay leaves, peppercorns, and vinegar in a large stockpot.
  3. Cover with cold water.
  4. Bring just to a boil.
  5. Reduce heat immediately to a very gentle simmer.
  6. Skim off any foam that rises during the first 30 minutes.
  7. Simmer uncovered for 4-8 hours. Add more water if the bones become exposed.
  8. Remove large bones and vegetables.
  9. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve.
  10. Cool quickly and refrigerate for up to 5 days or freeze for up to 6 months.

Notes

  • A well-made broth will often gel when chilled due to the collagen extracted from the turkey bones.
  • Optional additions: parsley stems, thyme, rosemary, or mushroom stems.
