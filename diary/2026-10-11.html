U.S. Senate Bean Soup

bean soup

References

Derived from U.S. Senate historical article: "Senate Bean Soup".

Ingredients

  • 4 lb dried navy beans
  • 3-4 lb smoked ham hocks
    • or 2 lb diced ham plus 2 ham hocks
  • 4 medium onions, finely chopped
  • 6 celery stalks, diced (optional)
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 qt water for soaking beans
  • 12 qt water or chicken stock for cooking
  • 4 Tbsp butter
  • 2 Tbsp salt, or to taste
  • 2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
  • 2 bay leaves

Instructions

Cook the Ham Hocks

  1. Place ham hocks in a 20+ quart stockpot.
  2. Add 12 quarts of water or stock.
  3. Simmer for 1½-2 hours until the meat is tender.

Sauté Vegetables

  1. Melt butter in a skillet.
  2. Sauté onions, celery, and garlic until softened.

Make the Soup

  1. Add beans and sautéed vegetables to the stockpot.
  2. Add bay leaves, black pepper, and cayenne (if using).
  3. Simmer gently for 2-3 hours, stirring occasionally.
  4. Cook until beans are very tender.

Finish

  1. Remove ham hocks from the pot.
  2. Pull meat from the bones and chop.
  3. Return meat to the soup.
  4. Discard bones, skin, and bay leaves.
  5. Season with salt and additional pepper to taste.

Notes

Yields approximately 14-16 quarts (60-70 one-cup servings)
