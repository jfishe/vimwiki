Beef and Bean Chili

chili beans ancho cascabel guajillo morita beef

Chile Purée

  • Stemmed and seeded:
    • 12 dried guajillo chiles,
    • 8 dried ancho chiles
    • 12 dried cascabel chiles
    • 8 dried morita chiles
  • 8 cups hot water for soaking
  • 1 tsp cloves
  • 3 Tb ground cumin
  • 2 Tb Mexican oregano
  • 2 tsp black pepper, whole or freshly ground
  • 16 cloves garlic, minced (1 Tb + 1 tsp granulated)
  • 4 tsp salt, plus more to taste

Chili Base

  • 8 lb ground beef (80/20)
    • 4 tsp baking soda dissolved in 4 Tb water
  • 4 large yellow onions, diced
  • 4 green bell peppers, diced
  • 8 tbsp (½ cup) tomato paste
  • 8 cans (14.5 oz each) crushed tomatoes
  • 8 cups beef broth (substitute 2 cups beer)
  • 4 tbsp brown sugar or piloncillo (optional)
  • 2 Tb bacon drippings or oil per pound of ground beef, as needed.
  • 4 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 4 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder

Beans

4 lb dried beans prepared per Instant Pot Dried Beans.

Directions

  1. Warm griddle to medium heat.
  2. Roast unpeeled garlic until soft, about 15 minutes.
  3. Cool, peel and roughly chop.
  4. For whole chiles, remove chile stems, split and shake out seeds.
  5. Toast both sides by pressing with a spatula on a medium griddle, avoid scorching.
    1. Rehydrate with water for 30 minutes
    2. Discard water
  6. Grind any whole spices
  7. Puree chiles and spices with minimal amount of broth.
  8. Strain out any remaining skins or seeds.
  9. Season with salt.
  10. Warm stock pot to medium-high and heat drippings.
  11. Mix the baking soda solution thoroughly into the raw beef.
  12. Rest for 15 minutes.
  13. Brown the beef in batches with onions.
  14. Add peppers and cook until softened.
  15. Drain excess fat.
  16. Stir in and cook chile purée to temper raw flavor.
  17. Add beans, Worcestershire, broth, beer, and tomatoes.
  18. Simmer gently for 1–2 hours.
  19. Add dark chocolate
  20. Add sugar and salt to taste.
